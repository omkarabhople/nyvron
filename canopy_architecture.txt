CANOPY - MACRO-STRATEGY ENGINE
Core UI/UX Architecture & Functional Specification
(Strictly Unseeded - Structural & Functional Logic Only)

=========================================
SCROLL 1: STRATEGIC COMMAND & SYLLABUS MATRIX
=========================================
[Panel] Macro Goal Countdown & Status
- Functioning: Calculates time delta between current date and target execution date.
- Data Bindings: 
  > Target Date: [Date Object] 
  > Days Remaining: [Dynamic Integer]
  > Global Readiness Score: [Dynamic Float Percentage] based on aggregated node mastery.

[Panel] Dynamic Syllabus Heatmap
- Functioning: Renders a dense matrix grid representing the entire syllabus/curriculum.
- Data Bindings: X/Y axes map to [Macro-Subjects] and [Sub-Topics]. Cell color intensity (alpha channel) is bound to [Confidence Score Float] (0.0 to 1.0).
- Interaction: Clicking a cell opens a detail popover listing the linked study materials and historical performance on that specific node.

=========================================
SCROLL 2: KNOWLEDGE GRAPH & INGESTION
=========================================
[Panel] Autonomous Ingestion Pipeline
- Functioning: Visualizes the OCR/NLP processing of uploaded documents (e.g., past papers, textbooks).
- Data Bindings: 
  > Ingestion Queue: [Array of Document Metadata]
  > Extraction Status: Progress bar bound to [Pages Processed / Total Pages].
  > Auto-Tagged Themes: [Array of Strings] dynamically populating as text analysis completes.

[Panel] Micro-Theme Dependency Graph
- Functioning: A force-directed node graph mapping relationships between concepts.
- Data Bindings: Nodes represent [Extracted Themes]; Edges represent [Algorithmic Relationships] (e.g., "Prerequisite to", "Frequently tested with"). Node size corresponds to [Weightage/Frequency Integer].
- Interaction: Scroll/pinch to zoom. Clicking a node highlights all its 1st and 2nd-degree connections, dimming the rest of the canvas.

=========================================
SCROLL 3: VELOCITY & PREDICTIVE TRAJECTORY
=========================================
[Panel] Burn-Down & Velocity Chart
- Functioning: Tracks actual progress against the required pace to complete the matrix by the target date.
- Data Bindings: 
  > Ideal Trajectory: Linear line from [Total Syllabus Volume] to 0.
  > Actual Trajectory: Plotted line based on [Completed Nodes] over [Time Series].
- Interaction: Hovering over the delta between the two lines displays an automated advisory (e.g., "Currently 4 days behind optimal pace").

[Panel] High-Weightage Alert Center
- Functioning: Algorithmic feed prioritizing study areas based on historical frequency and current user weakness.
- Data Bindings: List sorted by a computed score `([Historical Frequency] * (1 - [User Confidence]))`.

=========================================
SCROLL 4: ADAPTIVE ACTION PLAN
=========================================
[Panel] Tactical Resource Allocation
- Functioning: Recommends specific chapters or active recall sessions based on the current matrix gaps.
- Data Bindings: Generates [Action Item Objects] linking directly to the 'Library' or 'Cascara Timer'.
- Interaction: Includes a 1-click "Add to Today's Plan" button that dispatches the task to the global schedule state.
